Six Reasons to Cruise Bordeaux with Scenic

1. Once-in-a-lifetime experiences

Immerse yourself in elegant and unforgettable moments with Scenic Enrich. These exclusive encounters will take you into the heart of Bordeaux through art, culture and music.

2. France’s only over water cooking school

Bring culinary expertise into an interactive environment with Scenic Culinaire, only available with Scenic. Participate in guided cooking classes with produce sourced from Bordeaux’s markets.

3. Immersive, handpicked excursions

Flexible excursions allow you to personalise your day to suit your preferences. Opt for cognac tasting at the acclaimed estate of Rémy Martin one day and a guided e-bike tour the next.

4. The most luxurious suites

Scenic Diamond’s generously sized suites are elegantly appointed with premium amenities. Balcony, Junior and Royal Suites feature the signature Scenic Sun Lounge.

5. Truly all-inclusive luxury

We set the standard for 5-star truly all-inclusive ultra-luxury river cruising. We are best-in-class and every trip is our opportunity to raise the bar even higher.

6. A gastronomic journey

Savour delectable dishes using Bordeaux’s best ingredients with a number of dining options to choose from, along with included premium beverages throughout your river cruise.

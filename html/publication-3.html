Day 1 | Bordeaux

Welcome to Bordeaux, a UNESCO World Heritage site and one of the most desirable places to visit in France. On arrival, your friendly crew will be ready to welcome you on board the luxurious Scenic Diamond.

Dinner included

Day 2 | Bordeaux > Libourne

Spend the afternoon in Saint-Émilion, a World Heritage-listed village with world-famous wineries. Explore the unique Monolithic Church, which is mostly underground and carved directly into the limestone. In the evening, your Scenic Space-Ship will be docked overnight in Libourne, the winemaking capital of northern Gironde.

Breakfast, lunch & dinner included

Day 3 | Libourne > Pauillac

Choose from a walking tour of Libourne, wine tasting at a vineyard in Saint-Émilion, or Paint and Pour on the Sun Deck, where an artist will guide you through watercolour techniques as you sip on local wines and enjoy the magnificent river views.

Breakfast, lunch & dinner included

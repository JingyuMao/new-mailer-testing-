Day 7 | Bordeaux City

Tour Bordeaux with a guide or shop with a chef before an interactive demo in Scenic Culinaire. Today you’ll savour a tasting of fresh oysters, straight from the waters of Arcachon Bay. Tonight’s Scenic Enrich event is an after-hours show at Les Bassins des Lumières.

Breakfast, lunch & dinner included

Day 8 | Bordeaux > Bourg

Take a tram to the Cité du Vin Museum and enjoy an interactive journey of wine discovery. Alternatively, enjoy an exhilarating ride through Bordeaux in a motorcycle sidecar or visit the charming bayside town of Arcachon.

Breakfast, lunch & dinner included

Day 9 | Bourg

The fortified city of Cognac is renowned for producing some of the world’s finest brandy. Tour the breathtaking estate of Rémy Martin, before sampling some of the cognacs, or visit the Carriages Museum inside Bourg Castle.

Breakfast, lunch & dinner included

Day 4 | Pauillac > Fort Médoc

Travel to a prestigious winery by e-bike for a tasting. Tonight, your Scenic Enrich experience awaits. Château d’Agassac is one of the region’s oldest winemaking establishments and tonight, musicians perform classical masterpieces for your exclusive enjoyment.

Breakfast, lunch & dinner included

Day 5 | Fort Médoc > Cadillac

In the 17th century explorer Anthony Laumet, the Sieur de Cadillac, was born near Cadillac. He established the port of Fort Pontchartrain du Détroit, now known as Detroit, where the American automotive industry began. The Cadillac car is named in his honour.

Breakfast, lunch & dinner included

Day 6 | Cadillac > Bordeaux

Choose from a selection of Scenic Freechoice excursions, including tastings at a choice of vineyards, exploring Roquetaillade Castle or a visit to Château Toulouse-Lautrec, the former family home of renowned French artist Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec.

Breakfast, lunch & dinner included

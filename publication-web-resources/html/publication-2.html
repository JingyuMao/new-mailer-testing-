Unrivalled Moments

Our expert Journey Designers have crafted a range of immersive experiences to ensure your Beautiful Bordeaux river cruise creates memories that last a lifetime.

1. Classical Concert at Château d’Agassac

Soak in the ambiance of this fairytale property before a string quartet performs classical masterpieces for your enjoyment.

2. Visit the Rémy Martin estate

Tour the breathtaking estate of Rémy Martin, before sampling some of the cognacs.

3. Les Bassins des Lumières

Be welcomed with a glass of champagne at this impressive art installation and be immersed in the exhibit with an exclusive show.

4. Motorcycle sidecar experience

Weave through the streets of UNESCO World Hertiage-listed Bordeaux in a motorcycle side car.

5. E-Bike ride through the Médoc countryside

Cycle through the Médoc countryside that takes you past many grand aristocratic châteaux in the world’s leading red wine producing region and enjoy a tasting at one of the estates.

11 Day Beautiful Bordeaux

Renowned for producing some of the world’s finest wines, Bordeaux offers more than just exceptional vintages. During an 11 Day Beautiful Bordeaux river cruise, you’ll navigate the Gironde, Dordogne and Garonne rivers, as the region unfolds in lush vineyards and verdant landscapes.

This Bordeaux to Bordeaux roundtrip is complemented by exquisite French cuisine and, of course, you’ll cycle through idyllic sceneries, delve into winemaking, and participate in cooking classes. Beyond gastronomy, Bordeaux is rich in history, boasting elegant châteaux and ancient churches which you’ll explore with knowledgeable local guides. This picturesque region epitomises beauty and romance, providing a sensory feast that will live up to your every expectation.

We look forward to welcoming you on board, to create stories and memories to last a lifetime.

11 Day Beautiful Bordeaux Cruise Overview

Day 10 | Bourg > Blaye > Bordeaux

This morning you’ll sail towards Blaye, a small town with a rich military history, surrounded by vineyards known for red wine production. Most famously, this petite hamlet is home to a World Heritage-listed citadel that was constructed to protect the city of Bordeaux from enemy attacks. You’ll have a choice of excursions, from walking tours to cycling. Later, you’ll cruise back to Bordeaux for your final evening on board.

Breakfast, lunch & dinner included

Day 11 | Bordeaux

Today, your wonderful river cruise comes to an end. Disembark after breakfast and transfer to Bordeaux airport for your onward journey, taking a lifetime of wonderful memories with you.

Breakfast included

Please note: The Gironde, Dordogne and Garonne waterways are part of a tidal river system which results in changing water levels in the region. Every effort is made to keep to the brochure itinerary, however, based on tides it may be amended or modified.

Please note this day to day itinerary is based on 2027 departures, the day to day on other itineraries slightly differs on certain days. Please see our website for the itinerary.
